# Name, seal and look

The licence of this repository ([LICENSE](LICENSE), [LICENSE-EXCEPTION](LICENSE-EXCEPTION)) covers the code. It does not grant any right to the name **Kuutti**, to the seal emblem, or to the visual identity of the app, the website and the store listings. Those identify one service: the one run by the Kuutti association, where every account has been verified through Finnish bank identification and where the association answers for moderation and for personal data.

This matters more here than for most software. People decide whom to meet, and what to tell them, partly because the app in their hand is Kuutti. A copy that looks like Kuutti but is run by someone else, with other rules about identity and data, puts them at risk. That is a user-safety question before it is a branding one.

## What you may do

- Run, study, change and redistribute the code under the AGPL, including as a service of your own.
- Say truthfully that your project is based on Kuutti's source code, in running text, for example "based on the open-source code of Kuutti".
- Use the name to refer to this project when writing or speaking about it.

## What needs a different name and look

If you distribute a changed version, or run the code as a service, then:

- Give it its own name. Not "Kuutti", not a name that contains it or is easily confused with it.
- Use your own icon and emblem. Do not use the seal or a variation of it.
- Do not present it, in the app, on a website or in a store listing, as being Kuutti, as operated by the Kuutti association, or as endorsed by it.
- Keep the copyright and licence notices, and offer your source as the AGPL requires. The `SOURCE_URL` setting exists for that: point it at your repository (see `GET /health` and the app's "Source code" section).

## Status

This is the project's statement of intent, written by the maintainers. The name is not yet a registered trademark. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. The association will review this text and may register the name and the emblem; until then, the rules above are what we ask of anyone who builds on this code.
